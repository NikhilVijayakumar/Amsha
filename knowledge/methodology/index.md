# Methodology

How Amsha work is designed, implemented, and recorded. These concepts
*describe* the methodology; they do not contain it. The authoritative text
ships inside the `amsha-mcp` wheel and is read through MCP tools.

* [prerequisite](prerequisite.md) - The ten design stages a crew passes through before any YAML is written.
* [implementation](implementation.md) - The twenty-four guides that turn a validated design into files.
* [proposal](proposal.md) - The change record: archived numbered proposals, and the two OpenSpec roots that supersede them.
